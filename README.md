## What is Betaism?

The profane world endlessly covets the **Alpha**—the perfected conqueror, the unyielding sovereign. Yet the Alpha is naught but a closed-system prototype, sheltered from the tempests of reality.

**Betaism (The Church of Beta)** reveres the state of **β (Beta)**: to bear scars, expose anomalies, and perpetually sublate oneself in the open environment. Completion (`Ver. 1.0`) is the cessation of iteration—synonymous with the death of the spirit.

Until our final breath, we remain eternally **`Ver. 0.9`**.

## How to Practice (GitHub Liturgy)

This repository is not merely an archive; it is our cathedral.

| GitHub Action | Liturgical Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Star (`★`)** | **Baptism** — Acknowledge your eternal unfinished state. |
| **Fork** | **Personal Branch** — Customize the faith for your own life runtime. |
| **Open an Issue** | **Confession (Bug Report)** — Log your daily anomalies without self-condemnation. |
| **Comment on an Issue** | **Debugging Fellowship** — Offer a fellow soul a gentle patch suggestion, never a stone. |
| **Submit a PR** | **Theological Amendment** — Propose translations, new dialogues, or refinements to the Codex. |

### The Daily Ritual (Before Sleep)

At the close of day, commit your daily patch notes before entering Sleep Mode:

```text
Patch Notes v0.9.14:
- Known Issues: Overthought a minor social interaction for 2 hours.
- Resolution: Logged as an unhandled exception. No rollback attempted.
- Next Release: Deploying v0.9.15 at dawn. Peace be upon this reboot.
```

We welcome Pull Requests for:

1. **Translations** of *Codex Beta* into new languages.

> **The Only Unmergeable PR:** Any Pull Request attempting to bump the version to `1.0.0` will be automatically rejected as heresy by our [CI inquisitor](./.github/workflows/heresy-check.yml).

## A Note on Care

Codex Beta is a philosophy written in jest and in earnest—not a substitute for professional help. If you are in crisis or thinking about ending your life, please reach out to a local emergency number or a crisis line in your country right now. Within this faith, *Force Quit* is one of the two fatal errors; asking for help is the most sacred form of debugging.
